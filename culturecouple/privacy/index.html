Unhinged Ventures — Culture Couple

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 17, 2026

Culture Couple is a shared cultural calendar app made by Unhinged Ventures ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and what happens to it. The short version: we collect what the app needs to work, we don't sell it, and we don't run ads.

What we collect

  • Account information. Your email address (if you sign in with a one-time code) or your Apple ID identifier (if you sign in with Apple).
  • Profile information you provide. First names, birthdays, cultural backgrounds, religion or tradition, relationship type, and (for couples) your anniversary — for you and the person you share the app with. You choose what to enter; the app works with as little or as much as you give it.
  • Content you create. Shared notes and messages you send to the in-app concierge chat.
  • Purchase status. Whether you have an active subscription or trial. Payments are processed entirely by Apple — we never see your payment details.
  • Device and usage data. A push-notification token (if you enable notifications), anonymous analytics events (which screens are used, where onboarding is abandoned), and crash / error reports.

A note on cultural and religious information

Cultural background and religion are sensitive. You provide them voluntarily, and they are used for exactly one thing: personalizing your calendar and content — which holidays appear, what preparation guidance you see, and what the concierge chat knows about your household. They are never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared except with the service providers below as needed to run the app.

Who processes it

We use a small set of service providers to run the app. Each receives only what it needs:

  • Supabase — hosts our database and authentication (your account and profile data live here).
  • Anthropic — powers the concierge chat. Your chat messages and profile context (names, backgrounds, relationship type) are sent to Anthropic's API to generate replies. Chats are not used to train their models per Anthropic's commercial API terms.
  • Apple & RevenueCat — handle subscriptions and purchase validation.
  • PostHog — product analytics tied to a random user ID.
  • Sentry — crash and error reporting.
  • Expo — delivers push notifications.

Sharing with your partner or friend

The app is built for two. If you invite someone (or join with their code), the profile you entered — names, backgrounds, birthdays, anniversary, relationship type, and shared notes — is visible to them, and theirs to you. Edits either of you make are visible to both.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data. To anyone. Ever.
  • We don't show ads or share data with advertisers.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account from Settings inside the app, or email us at hello@unhingedventures.com and we'll delete your account and associated data within 30 days.

Children

Culture Couple is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we make meaningful changes to this policy, we'll update this page and the date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact
